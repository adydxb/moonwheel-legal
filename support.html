Support

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Need help with Moonwheel? Email aaentertainmentx@gmail.com.

For faster troubleshooting, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Moonwheel app version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome. Never email passwords or payment-card details.

Subscriptions and billing

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Apple’s subscription settings, choose Moonwheel, and follow Apple’s options. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore a purchase

Use “Restore Purchases” in Moonwheel while signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. If access is still missing, restart the app and email support with the product name and approximate purchase date. Do not send card details.

Refund or unfamiliar charge

Apple controls App Store billing and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Billing Support.

Downloads and offline playback

A track will not download

  • Confirm that your device is online and has enough free storage.
  • Keep Moonwheel open until the download completes.
  • Try the download again on a stable connection.

A downloaded track will not play

Remove that download in Moonwheel, then download it again. Also check the device volume, Silent Mode behavior, Focus settings, and whether another app is controlling audio.

Remove downloaded content

Use Moonwheel’s download controls where available. Uninstalling Moonwheel also removes its locally stored preferences and downloaded media from the device.

Playback and sleep timer

If audio stops unexpectedly, reopen Moonwheel and press play. Check whether the sleep timer completed, another app interrupted playback, or iOS ended background activity. When reporting a repeating issue, tell us whether it happened with the screen locked, headphones connected, or another audio app open.
